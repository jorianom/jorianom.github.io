## Users

Primary: tech recruiters and freelance clients evaluating John Jairo Riaño for software development roles or projects. Secondary: peers, academic community, and professional network. Audience is Spanish-speaking in Colombia/LATAM.

## Product Purpose

Professional portfolio that demonstrates John Jairo Riaño's skills, projects, experience, and credentials as a full-stack developer. Success means visitors understand his capability to own a product end-to-end and take action (contact, hire, connect).

## Positioning

Full-stack developer who bridges enterprise integration (NetSuite, .NET, Go) with modern frontend (React, Next.js). Can own a project from architecture through deployment — equally comfortable with backend logic, databases, cloud infrastructure, and developer tooling.

## Product Principles

1. **Content truth** — Never invent achievements, projects, or experience. Only improve clarity of what exists.
2. **Recruiter-first hierarchy** — Prioritize scanability and the signals hiring decision-makers need.
3. **Performance as feature** — Lighthouse metrics are a design constraint, not an afterthought.
4. **Accessible by default** — WCAG AA minimum, no exceptions. Must work with assistive technology.
5. **Purposeful motion** — Animation serves comprehension and feedback, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. Spanish-language audience. Must support keyboard navigation, screen readers, and sufficient color contrast. Dark theme must maintain readable contrast ratios.
